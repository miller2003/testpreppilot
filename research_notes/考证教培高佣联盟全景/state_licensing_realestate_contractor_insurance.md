# State Licensing Exam Prep Affiliate Programs: Real Estate, Contractor, Home Inspector/Appraiser, NMLS, Insurance, Securities (US, as of 2026-10-09)

Currency: all money figures are USD. "Official" = brand's own page; "3rd-party" = affiliate directory/aggregator (LinkClicky, affi.io, Lasso, FlexOffers listing, Affilitizer, AffPaying), which are often stale or conflicting. Research was done 2026-10-09 by web search + fetch; several official pages (McKissock) returned 403, so some numbers come only from search-result extracts of the official page.

## Q1. Real estate pre-licensing / exam prep: which brands have affiliate programs, terms, coverage?

### Takeaway
The two strongest open, self-serve real estate programs are **Aceable (AceableAgent/PrepAgent; in-house tracking, 15-25% tiered, 30-day window, claimed 70% intro for new partners)** and **Colibri Real Estate (ex-Real Estate Express; impact.com, 10-25% tiered, "up to $60/sale", 30-day window, AOV ~$200)**. McKissock (Colibri family) also advertises "up to $60/sale". The CE Shop appears only in 3rd-party listings (10%, 30-day, network status conflicting). Kaplan Real Estate Education and RealEstateU run partner/revenue-share programs aimed at brokerages/associations/schools with no published rate. No affiliate program found for Real Estate Exam Scholar, Champions School, or Mbition.

## Q2. Contractor licensing (CA CSLB, FL, TX, AZ, etc.): do high-ticket schools pay affiliates?

### Takeaway
No public affiliate program was found for any major CSLB school (Contractors State License Schools/CSLS, Contractors Intelligence School, Contractor Training Center, NCEP, examprep.org/American Contractors Exam Services). Published CSLB school prices found are $295-$695 — not "thousands" — though government fees + bond add materially. Only small regional self-serve programs exist (VA Contractor Training 15%, TradesmanCE 15%). The best path to contractor-license revenue is likely (a) direct outreach to CSLB schools for a negotiated CPA/lead deal, or (b) Colibri's Contractor Training Center via the Colibri relationship.

### Cited Findings
**Course prices (CA CSLB)**
- CSLS (Contractors State License Services) via Zack Academy: online-anytime + 2 days live $495; + 6 days live $695 — [Zack Academy listing](https://www.zackacademy.com/class/contractor-licensing-and-renewal/ca-contractor-in-school-licensing-online-anytime/65920); [6-day](https://www.zackacademy.com/class/contractor-licensing-and-renewal/ca-contractor-in-school-licensing-online-anytime-with-6-days-live-instruction/65924)
- Contractors Intelligence School: home study $295; online law & trade $495 (reg. $595); live online $695; "100% Pass Exam Money-Back Guarantee"; includes application review — [Contractors Intelligence School promo](https://promo.contractorsischool.com/online-contractors-license-school); [home course](https://promo.contractorsischool.com/contractors-home-course)
- CSLB: no educational requirement to get a license (school optional) — [CSLB guide PDF](https://cslb.ca.gov/Resources/GuidesAndPublications/BuildingYourCareerAsContractor.pdf)
- Current CSLB fees: original application $450; initial license $200 (sole owner) / $350 (non-sole owner); $25,000 contractor bond (+ $25,000 bond of qualifying individual if applicable) — [CSLB fee list](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/about_us/library/fees.aspx); [CSLB Issuing My License](https://cslb.ca.gov/contractors/applicants/contractors_license/exam_application/Issuing_My_License.aspx). Older $330/$15,000 figures in the CSLB guide and $300/$12,500 in the Zack listing are stale.
- SB 1455: exam fees now paid separately to PSI (application fee no longer covers initial exam) — [CSLB SB 1455 bulletin](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Resources/IndustryBulletins/2024/SB%201455%20changes.FINAL.pdf)

**Affiliate/referral programs**
- Search found no affiliate/referral terms for CSLS, NCEP (only unrelated "NCEP Fitness"), or Contractor Training Center; a coupon site's disclosure says it "may earn a commission via an affiliate agreement with Contractor Training Center" (suggests private deals exist, terms unknown) — [WeThrift CTC](https://www.wethrift.com/contractor-training-center)
- Contractor Training Center sells a Contractor License Application Processing Service and multi-state courses (e.g., NC) — [CTC application service](https://contractortrainingcenter.com/products/contractor-license-application-fulfillment-service); [CTC NC courses](https://contractortrainingcenter.com/collections/north-carolina-courses). CTC is a Richmond, VA firm, "national leader in contractor licensing" — [Business Wire May 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250514915557/en/Contractor-Training-Center-Launches-National-Program-to-Close-Construction-Skills-Gap-and-Position-Contractors-for-Growth). Search snippet described CTC as sister of Allied Schools / division of Gold Coast Schools (Colibri) — not verified on a primary page.
- Allied Schools (Colibri) sells CA contractor license prep — [Allied construction](https://www.alliedschools.com/construction/); Gold Coast sells FL contractor exam prep and application assistance — [Gold Coast GC prep](https://goldcoastschools.com/courses/construction/general-contractor/); [Gold Coast application assistance](https://goldcoastschools.com/courses/construction/application-assistance/)
- American Contractors Exam Services (examprep.org) claims prep in all 50 states; no affiliate page found — [examprep.org](https://www.examprep.org/)
- VAContractorTraining.com: 15% per purchase via link (Virginia) — [VA Contractor Training affiliate](https://www.vacontractortraining.com/affiliate)
- TradesmanCE (At Your Pace Online): 15%, PayPal — [Tradesmance affiliate](https://www.tradesmance.com/affiliate)
- GAPS Training (CGC/CGP certification): referral bonus paid after 6-week refund period; W-9 required — [GAPS Training](https://gapstraining.com/affiliate-referral-bonus-program/)
- NAHB BuilderBooks: 10% referral fee, but built for home builders associations, annual payout, Post Affiliate Pro — [NAHB BuilderBooks toolkit](https://www.nahb.org/Advocacy/Public-Toolkits/BuilderBooks-Retail-Affiliate-Toolkit)
- Contractor exam reference books are commonly monetized via Amazon Associates (e.g., open-exam-prep.com) — [open-exam-prep](https://open-exam-prep.com/books/trades/general-contractor)
- Legal note: CSLB restricts contractor referral services from soliciting/negotiating contracts on contractors' behalf (relevant only if monetizing contractor leads, not school referrals) — [JD Supra on CSLB FAST FACTS](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/california-contractors-state-license-23696/)

### Inferences
- "Thousands of dollars" CSLB packages were not found in published prices; total all-in cost (course $295-$695 + $450 app + PSI exam fees + license $200-$350 + bond premium) may approach ~$1,500+, which is perhaps what the owner saw. Some schools also bundle application filing/LLC services, raising ticket size.
- Because no CSLB school runs a public program, a direct pitch (e.g., flat $50-$150 per enrolled student or per qualified lead) to CSLS, Contractors Intelligence School, and CTC is the realistic route; numbers here are hypothetical negotiation targets, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No source for any CSLB school referral fee, CPL, or partner program; PSI exam fee amounts not retrieved.
- FL/TX/AZ-specific schools' affiliate status beyond Gold Coast not found.
- CTC application service price (page returned 429).
