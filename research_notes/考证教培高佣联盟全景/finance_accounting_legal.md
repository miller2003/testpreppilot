# Affiliate Programs for US Accounting, Finance and Legal Exam Prep (CPA/EA/CMA/CIA, CFA/FRM/CAIA/CFP, Securities, LSAT/Bar)

Research date: 2026-10-09. Currency: all figures USD unless noted. The networks/brands are US merchants, but no source stated currency explicitly beyond $ signs; treat % rates as % of order subtotal in USD.
Source-quality legend: [OFFICIAL] = brand's own page or the network's live merchant profile; [DIRECTORY] = third-party aggregator (FlexOffers, Lasso, AmaLinks, affi.io, Affilitizer, affsignal, FMTC). Directory data is often undated or stale and should be verified inside the network dashboard after approval.

## Q1. CPA / EA / CMA / CIA programs: commission, cookie, network, coverage

### Takeaway
Only two CPA-category programs have verifiable, currently live terms: UWorld (Awin, 10% default, 30-day cookie, covers CPA + CMA + CFA; plus a legacy UWorld Roger CPA LinkConnector campaign at 10% of subtotal) and Surgent (LinkConnector, managed by JEBCommerce, 10% baseline "with room for growth", 30-day cookie). Becker's only documented public rate is 1.6% and its FlexOffers listing is inactive; Gleim, Kaplan accounting, NINJA, Universal CPA, Lambers, Fast Forward Academy and HOCK show no public commission program (partnership/discount-only or not found).

## Q2. CFA / FRM / CAIA / CFP / securities programs

### Takeaway

UWorld's Awin program covers CFA L1-L3 at a 10% default. Kaplan Schweser, Bloomberg Exam Prep, Mark Meldrum, Dalton, Kaplan CFP, Fitch Learning and The American College show no public affiliate programs (society/university partnerships only).

## Q3. Legal: LSAT and Bar exam programs

### Takeaway
Legal is the weakest category for public affiliate programs. Verifiable options: Odyssey Test Prep (in-house LSAT program, 10% per referral, up to $1,200 on its $12,000 tutoring package, paid 30 days after purchase), Princeton Review (Awin, up to 6%, 10-day cookie, avg sale $990), Blueprint Prep (Impact, active since 2020; directory rate 6% with 1-day cookie on FlexOffers), and Kaplan corporate (FlexOffers 12% with restrictions). BARBRI (incl. PowerScore, AdaptiBar), Themis, 7Sage, LSAT Demon, LSATMax/BarMax (TestMax) and Quimbee show no public affiliate programs.

### Cited Findings
- Odyssey Test Prep: "Get paid 10% commission and earn up to $1200 for each successful referral"; covers LSAT tutoring, courses, admissions consulting; e.g., 80-hour in-person package ($12,000) pays $1,200; paid 30 days after purchase (aligned with non-refund policy); apply by email; target partners are pre-law advisors, tutors, consultants, content creators; cookie not specified; no date on page. [OFFICIAL] — [Odyssey affiliate program](https://odysseytestprep.com/affliate-program/); [Odyssey become an affiliate](https://odysseytestprep.com/become-an-affiliate/)
- A search snippet indicated Odyssey requires a minimum of 2 successful referrals before payment (not confirmed on the fetched page). — [Odyssey become an affiliate](https://odysseytestprep.com/become-an-affiliate/)
- The Princeton Review on Awin: up to 6% commission, 10-day cookie, 270-day locking period, average sale $990 (some page stats cite a 2015 survey). [OFFICIAL network profile] — [Awin Princeton Review](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/18466); FlexOffers 5%/10 days and Lasso 8%/10 days/$50 threshold conflict. [DIRECTORY] — [FlexOffers Princeton Review](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/the-princeton-review-affiliate-program); [Lasso Princeton Review](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/princeton-review/)
- Blueprint Prep (LSAT, also MCAT etc.): Active on Impact since 2020-03-09 [DIRECTORY] — [Affilitizer Blueprint](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/blueprintprep.com); FlexOffers lists 6% per sale with 1-day cookie (affi.io marks the FlexOffers listing Closed; a Sale Gains entry shows 30-day cookie). — [FlexOffers Blueprint](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/blueprint-prep-affiliate-program/); [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/income)
- 7Sage: no affiliate program; has a campus ambassador program (free 7Sage Live + per-semester cash stipend; forum post, undated) and 7Sage Rewards customer credit. — [7Sage ambassador post](https://7sage.com/discussion/62003/become-a-7sage-ambassador-priority-applications-due-9-1); [7Sage rewards](https://7sage.com/discussion/40818/7sage-rewards/)
- LSAT Demon: only campus pre-law society discounts (e.g., 25% off first month). — [Stanford Pre-Law resources](https://prelaw.groups.stanford.edu/programs/resources-and-partnerships)
- PowerScore is now owned by BARBRI; no affiliate program found. AdaptiBar is "a BARBRI company"; no affiliate program found. — [BARBRI: PowerScore](https://www.barbri.com/en/resources/powerscore-your-lsat-success-partner); [BARBRI AdaptiBar supplements](https://www.barbri.com/en/adaptibar-supplements)

Prices (possibly stale): BARBRI base $2,995; Themis $2,745 live / $2,495 on-demand. — [Brooklyn Law disclosure](https://www.brooklaw.edu/media/0nhcvckc/msche-third-party-provider-disclosure.pdf); [CU Law bar review comparison](https://www.colorado.edu/law/comparing-commercial-bar-review-courses)
- Quimbee Bar Review: $1,199 one-time ($1,099 ABA Premium); its Refer-A-Friend program is "now retired"; no affiliate program found. — [Quimbee bar review cost](https://help.quimbee.com/how-much-does-quimbee-bar-review-cost)

### Gaps
- No public commission data for BARBRI, Themis, Kaplan Bar specifically, UWorld MBE, AdaptiBar, Quimbee, BarMax, 7Sage, LSAT Demon, PowerScore, LSATMax.
- Blueprint's current Impact rate/cookie only visible after applying.

Highest documented absolute payouts: Odyssey (up to $1,200 per LSAT tutoring referral, but niche and in-house) and Surgent (~$256+ per CPA sale at 10% baseline, upside via JEBCommerce tiers).
