# Affiliate Programs for US Academic Admissions, Teacher Certification, Public Safety / Civil Service, and Trades / Transportation / Safety Exams (as of 2026-10-09)

Currency: all figures are USD unless marked otherwise (Preply's Awin UK offer is in GBP). "FlexOffers" = FlexOffers.com network listing pages, which are third-party listings that can lag the advertiser's real terms. Many listings carry no date, so check every number against the live network terms before relying on it.

Network housekeeping that affects several programs below: ShareASale no longer exists as a standalone network. Awin moved all active ShareASale customers over by 2025-08-15, and ShareASale shut down on 2025-10-06 ([Hello Partner](https://hellopartner.com/shareasale-to-sunset-in-us-as-awin-migration-wraps-up/); [Brex charge finder](https://www.brex.com/tools/charge-finder/shareasale)). Commission and cookie terms carried over unless the advertiser changed them. Some accounts did not migrate, and publishers had to rejoin some programs manually ([Awin FAQ](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/ShareASale-to-Awin-platform-upgrade-FAQ)). Any program described below as "on ShareASale" (PrepScholar, Target Test Prep, OSHA Outreach Courses) should now be looked up on Awin.

## Q1. Academic admissions and test prep (PSAT/SAT/ACT/AP, GRE/GMAT, GED/HiSET, ASVAB)

### Takeaway
Big-brand academic test prep pays low percentages, roughly 3% to 12%, but on high order values: Princeton Review averages $990 per sale and Kaplan is reported at about $1,200. ASVABer (52%) and Mometrix (30%) are the high-percentage outliers. Mometrix covers by far the most exams on this site's list. No GED- or HiSET-specific program was found.

### Cited Findings
**Kaplan (Kaplan North America)**
- FlexOffers lists 12% of sales, a 30-day cookie, "restrictions apply," and coverage of "over 120 tests" plus certifications. The listing was last updated 2026-08-14 and shows EPC of about $1.12–$2.12 — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/kaplan-test-prep-affiliate-program/)
- Other listings conflict. UpPromote lists 5% per sale, CPS, with a 30-day cookie — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/kaplan/). Other FlexOffers mirrors show 5% or 8% (search snippets only).
- Affilimate says the average purchase "upwards of $1,200," a third-party claim — [Affilimate](https://affilimate.io/programs/kaplan-affiliate-program/)

**The Princeton Review (Awin merchant 18466)**
- Pays up to 6% on courses, tutoring, admissions counseling and homework help. The rate is 5% for incentive partners, 4% for subnetworks, and 0% on books and free events.
- Cookie is 10 days. Average sale is $990, and there is a 270-day locking period.
- Transactions stay pending until the course start date. Coverage includes SAT, ACT, GRE, GMAT, MCAT, LSAT and CFA L1 — [Awin](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/18466)
- Third-party listings disagree: Lasso shows 8% with a 10-day cookie, and FlexOffers shows 5% — [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/princeton-review/); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/the-princeton-review-affiliate-program)

**Magoosh**
- Official terms: runs on Impact. Content and community partners "typically start at 10%," and coupon/loyalty partners get about 1%. Attribution window is 30 days. Approval requires describing your site and audience. Named exams are GRE, GMAT, SAT and ACT "and more" — [Magoosh](https://magoosh.com/partner-with-us)
- FlexOffers shows 8% excluding free trials — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/magoosh-affiliate-program/). Acceleration Partners shows 10% with a 30-day cookie — [Acceleration Partners](https://www.accelerationpartners.com/affiliate-programs/magoosh)
- No source supports the 35% figure.

**PrepScholar (SAT/ACT/GRE)**
- Official terms: 10% commission and a 30-day cookie. Any purchase within 30 days counts, even if the customer comes back directly.
- Tracking was via ShareASale, managed by AffiliateManager.com — [PrepScholar partner page](https://www.prepscholar.com/sat/s/partner)
- Affilitizer shows the program active on Awin since July 2025, which fits the ShareASale-to-Awin migration — [Affilitizer](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/prepscholar.com)

**Target Test Prep (GMAT/GRE)**
- The binding agreement is hosted on Referral Rock. The counterparty is 99th Score Tutoring LLC d/b/a Target Test Prep, and covered products are GMAT and GRE prep — [Referral Rock terms](https://targettestprep.referralrock.com/v2/1/terms)
- Commission figures conflict:
  - FlexOffers: 8%, or 1.6%–12% by transaction, with a 60-day cookie (another bullet says 30) — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/target-test-prep-affiliate-program)
  - LinkClicky: 10% via ShareASale with a 60-day cookie — [LinkClicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/target-test-prep/)
  - Awin: 30-day attribution — [Awin](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/92143)

**Manhattan Prep**
- 3% per sale on Rakuten Advertising, US, per a third-party directory — [LinkClicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/manhattan-prep/)
- Shown as active on one network, listed May 2024 — [Affilitizer](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/manhattanprep.com)

**Economist GMAT Tutor**
- FlexOffers lists 12% of purchases under $2,500 and 14.4% above $2,500. This comes from a search snippet only; the page was not fetched and the product's current status was not verified — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/economist-gmat-tutor-affiliate-program)

**Prep Expert (SAT/ACT)**
- Official page says 10% with a 60-day cookie — [Prep Expert](https://prepexpert.com/affiliates/)
- FlexOffers contradicts this with 2.4%–4% of sales — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/prep-expert-affiliate-program/)

**ASVABer (ASVAB)**
- FlexOffers lists "52% on sales." Cookie and price are not stated. The listing was last updated 2026-06-30. The product has 2,000+ practice questions, videos and an AFQT estimator — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/asvaber-affiliate-program)

**Pocket Prep (130+ certifications)**
- 15% recurring commission on subscriptions, 45-day cookie, paid monthly via PayPal, onboarded through Impact. This is from the search snippet; the page returned 404 when fetched — [Pocket Prep](https://www.pocketprep.com/?p=7998)

**BenchPrep (included civil service, police and fire entrance exams)**
- FlexOffers shows 12% plus $12 per converted trial and $14.40 per converted subscription, with a 30-day cookie, but marks the program **deactivated** — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/benchprep-affiliate-program)

**Achievable**
- "20%" appears only on an unreliable AI-tools directory hosted on a traefik.me development domain. Treat it as unverified — [directory](https://rightai-rightai-nextjs-9gaxj1-0de616-74-48-65-149.traefik.me/ai/achievable-me)

**TestPrep-Online (K-12 gifted, placement, SAT, TEAS, STAAR)**
- Up to 25% of revenue, with commission on any purchase made through the link. Paid monthly via PayPal. Cookie is not stated. It does not cover civil service, police, ASVAB or teacher exams — [TestPrep-Online](https://www.testprep-online.com/affiliates)

### Inferences
- In dollars per sale, Kaplan (12% × roughly $1,000+) and Princeton Review (6% × $990, about $59) likely match or beat the 30%-on-$40–$80 study-guide programs. Princeton Review's 10-day cookie and pay-after-course-start rule both reduce realized revenue.
- For PSAT/SAT/ACT/AP the best fits are Kaplan, Princeton Review, PrepScholar, Magoosh and Prep Expert. For GRE/GMAT: Magoosh, TTP, Kaplan and Princeton Review. For ASVAB: ASVABer, plus Mometrix and Kaplan as broad backups.

### Gaps
- No affiliate program found for e-GMAT, Union Test Prep, GED Testing Service / GED Flash, HiSET, Khan Academy (expected) or Albert/UWorld.
- Kaplan's current published rate cannot be pinned down (5%, 8% or 12% depending on listing). Kaplan GED-specific terms were not found.
- No primary-source terms for Achievable or ASVABer; both are FlexOffers or directory-only.

**Wyzant**

- Wyzant's own FAQ describes payment when a referred student "completes the lead process" (messaging a tutor). This conflicts with the lessons-based model — [Wyzant support](https://support.wyzant.com/?p=1200)
- Revshare: 8% on first purchase, lifetime duration — [Revshare](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/wyzant-027b2163)

**Preply**

### Inferences
- Varsity Tutors is the best fit for SAT/ACT/AP pages, but whether it is still active and on which network (Impact) needs checking.

### Gaps
- No affiliate program found for Tutor.com.
- Dollar value per student for Varsity Tutors and Wyzant is not published.

## Q3. Teacher certification (Praxis, CBEST/CSET, TExES, FTCE, edTPA) and alternative certification

### Takeaway
Mometrix (30%, its own platform, very broad catalog) is the only verified high-percentage teacher-certification program. 240 Tutoring is reportedly on CJ, but its rate is unverified (30% or 50%). Alternative certification programs either pay only small consumer referral rewards (iteach: $50 gift card) or have no public program at all (Teachers of Tomorrow).

### Cited Findings
**Mometrix**
- The affiliate portal says "Pay-Per-Sale 30% for each sale you deliver." Minimum payout is $10. Payment is monthly, two months in arrears (January sales paid in March). Tracking is in-house software. Joining is free — [ref.mometrix.com](https://ref.mometrix.com/)
- The main affiliate page says "commissions of 30% or more" and directs applicants to a contact form. Cookie length is not stated — [mometrix.com/aff.htm](https://www.mometrix.com/aff.htm)
- The catalog includes Praxis, GRE, NBPTS, PMP and nursing, among others, judging from Mometrix Academy URLs such as [Praxis](https://www.mometrix.com/academy/praxis/) and [GRE](https://www.mometrix.com/academy/gre).

**240 Tutoring**
- A third-party directory lists the program on CJ with a 30-day cookie. It contradicts itself, saying "Typically around 30%" in one place and "50% per sale" in its FAQ. The page is undated except for © 2026 — [The Affiliate Monkey](https://theaffiliatemonkey.com/affiliate/240-tutoring-affiliate-program/)

**Study.com (Praxis and other teacher certification prep)**
- FlexOffers: 8% per sale, 7-day cookie. The listing may be closed — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/study-affiliate-program/)
- affi.io shows a closed Partnerize program at 3% and a Sale Gains program, open, with a 30-day cookie — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/study-affiliate-program)

**iteach (Texas and other alternative certification)**
- "Give $50, Get $50": the referrer gets a $50 Amazon gift card when the friend enrolls, and the friend gets $50 off. This is a consumer referral, not an affiliate program. The page is undated — [iteach](https://iteach.net/?p=17583)

**Teachers of Tomorrow**
- No referral or affiliate program was found. Searches returned only reviews and partner pages — [SimpleK12 review](https://www.simplek12.com/blog/teachers-tomorrow-review)

**NavaED / Kathleen Jasper, Teachers Test Prep, Certify Teacher, Teacher Ready**
- No affiliate program was found. Kathleen Jasper's NavaED-branded FTCE guide exists — [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/87340739)

### Inferences
- Mometrix is the only verified, primary-sourced high-percentage program that covers teacher certification alongside civil service, ASVAB and many trades. It is the single best "one program, many pages" choice in this research.
- High-ticket alternative certification programs ($4,000–$5,000) do not appear to run public affiliate programs. A direct business-development outreach for per-enrollment bounties could be tried, but none was documented.

### Gaps
- Mometrix cookie duration and exact exam list were not confirmed on the affiliate page.
- 240 Tutoring's real rate is unconfirmed; check CJ directly.
- No information found on Google Certified Educator prep affiliates, or on edTPA, CBEST or CSET specialists.

## Q4. Civil service and public safety (police, corrections, firefighter, NYC DCAS, postal)

### Takeaway
JobTestPrep is the strongest verified program here: 20%+ in the US, it covers police, firefighter, FBI, civil service and USPS, and pays on any purchase made through the link. Its weakness is a reported 7-day cookie on Awin. Mometrix is the broad backup. No police-, fire- or postal-only publisher program was found.

### Cited Findings
**JobTestPrep (US)**
- Official page: "Commission starting at 20% for every purchase that comes from your web assets," including products other than the one linked.
- Paid monthly, PayPal for small volumes. No fee to join, and the site should target job seekers or related audiences. Non-US affiliates are allowed.
- The site sells police, FBI, firefighter, civil service and USPS prep (for example "Free USPS Test" and "FDNY Exam") — [JobTestPrep](https://www.jobtestprep.com/affiliates)
- Awin US listing: 20% commission, **7-day cookie** — [Awin](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/18972)
- UK sister program: starts at 25% — [JobTestPrep UK](https://www.jobtestprep.co.uk/affiliate)

**BenchPrep**
- Previously covered police, firefighter and civil service exams at 12%; now deactivated on FlexOffers — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/benchprep-affiliate-program)

**Compliance note**
- The Massachusetts civil service office "does not recommend or endorse any third-party private business." Pages promoting prep should avoid implying government endorsement — [Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/guides/examination-guides-and-reading-lists)

### Inferences
- JobTestPrep's 7-day cookie, compared with Mometrix's unstated cookie, matters for users who research and then come back later. Testing both on police and postal pages makes sense.

### Gaps
- No affiliate program found for Police Test Guide, PoliceExamPrep, FireTEAM / Fire Test Prep / Fireprep.com, McCann, or Postal Exam 474 specialists.
- No program found for NYC DCAS-specific prep.

## Q6. CDL (general, air brakes, hazmat) and trucking lead generation

### Takeaway
No public, network-listed CPL offer for CDL school enrollment or driver recruiting was found. Available CDL affiliate programs pay a percentage on cheap ELDT and permit courses: DLA Academy 25% (about $3.75–$12.50 per sale), MyImprov 20%–50%, and CDL Schools USA "up to 20%." The real high-dollar opportunity is per-placed-driver recruiting fees: $250–$500 for a company driver and $600–$800 for an owner-operator. These require recruiter-style arrangements rather than standard affiliate links.

### Cited Findings
**DLA Academy (ELDT)**
- 25% commission, no caps. Payout per sale:
  - Single ELDT course (Hazmat, CDL A/B Theory, Passenger, School Bus): $5.00 on a $20 sale
  - CDL-A Permit Prep: $3.75 on a $15 sale
  - CDL A + B + Permit bundle: $11.25 on $45
  - H + P + S endorsement bundle: $12.50 on $50
- 30-day cookie with email match across devices. Paid weekly via PayPal or Stripe, no minimum, no application. No brand bidding allowed — [DLA Academy](https://www.academyofdla.com/products/communities/cdl-affiliate-program)

**CDL Schools USA**
- "Up to 20% commission," paid monthly, aimed at recruiters and influencers. The commission base is unspecified — [CDL Schools USA](https://www.cdlschoolsusa.com/partnership)

**MyImprov (defensive driving, driver's ed, CDL licensing courses)**
- 20% of the course price after fees, rising to 50% based on monthly volume (tiers not published). Uses a cookie with an IP-match fallback. Paid monthly by check, ACH or PayPal — [MyImprov](https://myimprov.com/affiliate)

**Driver recruiting economics (buyer side)**
- CDL Driver Leads recruiter placement pay: $250–$500 per company-driver placement and $600–$800 per owner-operator, paid after the driver has worked 2 weeks — [CDL Driver Leads](https://cdldriverleads.com/)
- CDL Lead Source sells 100 leads for $99 (about $0.99 per lead) — [CDL Lead Source](https://cdlleadsource.com/)
- A vendor blog reports direct leads at $13.22 versus $24 from third-party vendors. This is the vendor's own marketing claim — [FusionNow](https://fusionnow.io/2025/10/24/how-direct-lead-generation-helps-carriers-hire-truck-drivers-faster-and-for-less/)
- CDL-A Jobs USA pays "per qualified lead" via invoice or CPL network, amount not stated — [CDL-A Jobs USA](https://www.cdlajobsusa.com/employers-partnerscdl-a-driver-leads-for-employers-recruiting-partners)
- Liquid Trucking reports $6–$8 per lead, trending to $4 — [FleetOwner](https://www.fleetowner.com/perspectives/ideaxchange/blog/55403905/how-liquid-trucking-uses-full-funnel-recruiting-to-attract-qualified-drivers)
- Randall-Reilly (older analysis): cost per approved application of $1,100 via Facebook versus about $650 on other channels — [Randall-Reilly](https://randallreilly.com/drivers/articles-drivers/driver-recruiting-quantity-vs-quality-of-leads/)

### Inferences
- Carriers pay a lot per approved application ($650–$1,100 historically) but little per raw lead ($1–$24). So a testpreppilot CDL page would earn far more by sending qualified applicants to a recruiter or carrier under a per-hire or per-approved-application deal than from course commissions. That requires direct business development, not a self-serve network.
- CDL course affiliate payouts are small because ELDT courses are cheap ($15–$50).

### Gaps
- No verified CPA-network offers (MaxBounty, CPAlead, Perform[cb]) with a stated CDL or driver-job payout were found.
- No CDL school (CDL training school chains, carrier-sponsored schools) per-enrollment bounty was found.
- CDL Prep app and Driving-Tests.org affiliate terms were not found.
