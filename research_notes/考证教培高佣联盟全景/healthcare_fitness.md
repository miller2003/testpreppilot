# Healthcare / Nursing / Allied Health / Fitness Certification Affiliate Programs (US) — as of 2026-10-09

Scope note: about 23 searches and fetches. Commission figures come from brand pages where those exist. Otherwise they come from network or aggregator listings (FlexOffers, Lasso, LinkClicky, RevShare, Affilitizer), which are often stale or contradict each other. All amounts are USD unless stated otherwise. Any brand not listed below with a figure has **no verified figure** (see Gaps).

## Q1. Nursing exam-prep affiliate programs (NCLEX, TEAS, HESI, FNP)

### Takeaway
Most nursing prep brands pay 8–15% per sale, or don't publish a rate. The best-documented terms are **Lecturio at 15%** (official page, 30-day cookie) and **SimpleNursing at 8%** (FlexOffers, 30-day cookie). **Archer Review** has an in-house program that requires 1,000+ social followers and publishes no rate. **UWorld** is on Awin with a 30-day cookie, but its public description centres on accounting prep and gives no nursing rate.

### Cited Findings
- **Archer Review:** in-house program run through affiliates.archerreview.com. The page promises "competitive" commissions on every sale through your link, but gives no rate or cookie length. Requirement: "You must have at least 1,000 social media followers in order to apply." Covered exams per the page: NCLEX, TEAS, FNP certification, USMLE. Affiliates must disclose with #affiliate or #ArcherReviewPartner. — [Archer Review affiliates](https://www.archerreview.com/affiliates); terms: [affiliates.archerreview.com TOS](https://affiliates.archerreview.com/programs/archer-review-nursing/tos/). A search summary of the TOS says commissions are paid **quarterly** and are forfeited if the terms are breached. This was not verified by a direct fetch.
- **UWorld:** the Awin merchant profile describes a UWorld US affiliate program for exam prep, with accounting (CPA/CMA) as the example, and a **30-day attribution period**. No nursing-specific rate was published. — [Awin merchant 120129](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/120129)
- **UWorld NCLEX price:** "starts at $149 for a 30-day subscription". — [NSNA / UWorld](https://www.nsna.org/membership-benefits/uworld)
- **Lecturio (medical and nursing):** **15% of each paid subscription** bought through your link or promo code. **30-day** tracking. Paid monthly around the 15th via PayPal, Wise or wire, with a **$25 minimum**. Applications are reviewed within 7 days. An older Lecturio page describes a "bonus" without a percentage, so the two pages conflict. — [Lecturio affiliate (en)](https://www.lecturio.com/en/elearning/more-info/affiliate); [lecturio.com/affiliate](https://lecturio.com/affiliate)
- **SimpleNursing:** **8% per sale, 30-day cookie** on FlexOffers. Also tracked on Impact. Affilitizer shows an Impact listing dated Apr 2024 and a CPS listing dated Jul 2025. — [FlexOffers SimpleNursing](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/simplenursing-affiliate-program/); [Affilitizer SimpleNursing](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/simplenursing.com). There is also an in-house social affiliate page at simplenursing.com/social-affiliate/, per [RevShare "Best affiliate programs for nurses" (updated May 2026)](https://www.revshare.so/programs/best-affiliate-programs-for-nurses).
- **BoardVitals:** listed as a nursing affiliate (NCLEX and NP certification question banks). The RevShare guide gives no rate or cookie. — [RevShare nurses guide, May 2026](https://www.revshare.so/programs/best-affiliate-programs-for-nurses)
- **Nurse.com "Scrub Squad"** (CE courses and memberships, not NCLEX prep): code-based, **10% commission** on each purchase made with your code. — [Nurse.com Scrub Squad](https://www.nurse.com/scrub-squad/)
- **Picmonic:** in-house program paying commission on Picmonic Premium purchases, with "no limits on earnings". No % published. Perks include free Picmonic time, Amazon gift cards and swag. Aimed at college students and healthcare workers. — [Picmonic affiliates](https://www.picmonic.com/affiliates)
- **Kaplan (Nursing, MCAT and other prep):** FlexOffers shows **5% of sales** (an earlier snapshot showed 12%) with a **30-day cookie**. UpPromote lists 5%. Affilimate says "up to 2.4% base". Affilitizer shows Kaplan on GLOPSS, active since Apr 2025. — [FlexOffers Kaplan Test Prep](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/kaplan-test-prep-affiliate-program/); [UpPromote Kaplan](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/kaplan/); [Affilimate Kaplan](https://affilimate.io/programs/kaplan-affiliate-program/); [Affilitizer Kaplan](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/kaplan.com)

### Inferences
- At 8–15% on products priced around $100–$300 (for example UWorld NCLEX from $149), nursing prep likely pays roughly **$10–$45 per sale**. This is not high-ticket. Lecturio at 15% is the cleanest self-serve option.
- Archer Review covers NCLEX, TEAS, FNP and USMLE, which is the broadest single nursing and medical brand found. But its 1,000-follower rule is aimed at social creators, so an SEO site would need to argue for approval or apply through the program manager.

### Gaps
- No public affiliate terms were found for **ATI, Hurst Review, Mometrix (nursing), NurseAchieve, Level Up RN, NRSNG/Nursing.com, or Med-Challenger**. Searches turned up no affiliate page or network listing for them.
- UWorld nursing commission % is unknown. Joining on Awin is needed to see it.
- Archer Review rate and cookie are unpublished.

## Q2. Allied health / high-ticket career training ($1k–$4k): Penn Foster, USCI, Ed2Go, CareerStep, MedCerts, NHA, AAPC, Stepful

### Takeaway
No published per-enrollment commission or CPL figure was found for any high-ticket allied-health trainer. **Penn Foster** runs a manager-gated affiliate program that offers "link-out" and "host-and-post" (lead-gen style) campaigns on NET30 terms, but its rates are private. The only **Ed2Go** figure (8%, 30-day cookie) comes from a FlexOffers listing that says the program is **no longer offered**. **CareerStep** and **MedCerts** appear to have only B2B, school or employer partnerships.

### Cited Findings
- **Penn Foster:** apply through a contact form to the affiliate program manager. Features listed: "multiple payment options", **NET30 payment terms**, **link-out campaigns and host/post campaigns**, and "open sharing of data". No commission is published. — [Penn Foster affiliate contact](https://pennfoster.edu/affiliate/contact-us)
- **Ed2Go (Cengage):** FlexOffers lists **8% of sales, 30-day cookie**, but marks the program as not currently offered in its system. Ed2Go's 2026 partner flyer targets institutions that resell Ed2Go programs under their own brand, not referral affiliates. — [FlexOffers ed2go](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/ed2go-affiliate-program); [ed2go Partner Overview Flyer (2026-06)](https://partner.ed2go.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ed2go-Partner-Overview-Flyer.pdf)
- **CareerStep:** its partner route is academic partnerships (CareerStep.com/partner), and colleges resell its courses. No affiliate program was found. — [BusinessWire CareerStep](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210912005003/en); [Wake Tech CareerStep](https://www.waketech.edu/programs-courses/non-credit/workforce-training/online-learning/careerstep)
- **MedCerts:** "MedCerts Partner Solutions" serves employers, academic institutions and workforce agencies (B2B). No affiliate commission was found. — [IntuitionLabs MedCerts profile](https://intuitionlabs.ai/software/pdfs/medcerts.pdf)

### Inferences
- Penn Foster's "host/post" wording is the language of lead generation (a form posted to the advertiser). That suggests a **CPL or per-enrollment deal** is negotiable, but this is unverified. It is the most promising route for high-ticket medical assistant, phlebotomy and pharmacy tech traffic.
- For high-ticket allied-health traffic, the realistic paths are (a) direct deals with the program manager (Penn Foster) or (b) education lead-gen networks. Network-listed self-serve programs in this group are scarce or dead.

### Gaps
- No affiliate terms were found for **US Career Institute, AAPC (CPC), NHA (CCMA/CPT/ExCPT), Stepful, ProTrain, or Coursera health certificates** in this pass. Coursera's general affiliate program was out of scope and not checked here.
- No CPL figures were found for any allied-health trainer.

## Q3. EMT / medical / MCAT / USMLE prep (Pocket Prep, Amboss, Osmosis, Boards & Beyond, Blueprint, Kaplan)

### Takeaway
**Pocket Prep** is the best multi-exam app program. An earlier official page stated **15% recurring on subscriptions with a 45-day cookie** via Impact. The current 2026 partner page shows tiers (Starter, Growth, Impact, Elite) and withholds rates until you are accepted. **Lecturio** pays 15% on a 30-day cookie and covers medical and nursing. **Kaplan** (MCAT, USMLE, NCLEX) shows 5% with a 30-day cookie on FlexOffers.

### Cited Findings
- **Pocket Prep (earlier terms):** "15% Commission Rate", recurring on subscription products, **45-Day Cookie Window**, paid monthly via PayPal, joined through **Impact**. — [Pocket Prep affiliate page](https://www.pocketprep.com/?p=7998); [LinkClicky Pocket Prep](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/pocket-prep/) also says "15% initial, 15% per recurring". Lasso estimates about **$4.50 per sale** on an average app price of about $30. — [Lasso Pocket Prep](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/pocket-prep/)
- **Pocket Prep (current 2026 page):** four tiers. Starter and Growth have a "Competitive base commission", while Impact and Elite unlock a "Higher commission tier". The full structure is "shared upon acceptance". Commission is paid on a new Premium subscription **plus the first renewal**. Payout via direct deposit or PayPal with a $10 minimum. Schools are excluded. Responses come within 1–3 business days. This page names no network and no cookie length. — [Pocket Prep become-an-affiliate](https://get.pocketprep.com/become-an-affiliate/welcome)
- **Conflict:** the 15%/45-day terms come from the older page. The newer tiered page is vaguer, and its "first renewal only" wording contradicts the earlier "per recurring subscription". — same two sources above.
- **Kaplan MCAT/USMLE:** see Q1. 5% with a 30-day cookie on FlexOffers, conflicting with 12% and 2.4% figures elsewhere. — [FlexOffers Kaplan](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/kaplan-test-prep-affiliate-program/)
- **Lecturio:** 15% with a 30-day cookie (see Q1). — [Lecturio affiliate](https://www.lecturio.com/en/elearning/more-info/affiliate)

### Inferences
- Pocket Prep likely spans many healthcare certifications, which fits the site's long-tail exam pages. But at an app price of around $30 per sale it pays only a few dollars. Its value is breadth, not high payouts per sale. (The fetched partner page did not list exam coverage, apart from a CCRN testimonial.)

### Gaps
- No terms were found for **Amboss, Osmosis, Boards & Beyond, Blueprint MCAT, Med-Challenger, or dedicated EMT/NREMT prep brands** (searches surfaced no affiliate pages). Pocket Prep's exact exam list and current tier percentages were not verified.

## Q4. Fitness / personal trainer / nutrition certifications (NASM, ISSA, ACE, NCSF, AFPA, NESTA, Trainer Academy, Precision Nutrition)

### Takeaway
On percentage, **NCSF (15%, official page, Impact)** and **ISSA (5–14.4%, conflicting listings)** lead the verifiable programs. The undated **NESTA MMA division** release claims **30%** on certifications plus 30% on memberships, which is the highest rate found but is stale. **NASM** now appears to pay a flat **$4 per sale** (FlexOffers and RevShare, updated Aug 2026), down from the $25 flat rate in older blog posts. **AFPA** pays 5% and requires affiliates to be AFPA-certified. PTPioneer discloses only **CJ, Rakuten and Amazon** as networks.

### Cited Findings
- **NASM:** FlexOffers shows **$4 per online sale, 30-day cookie**, excluding sales with blacklisted promo codes. RevShare also shows $4 with a 30-day cookie and was "last updated August 31, 2026". Older blog roundups claim a **$25 fixed commission with a 30-day cookie**, which is likely stale. — [FlexOffers NASM](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/national-academy-sports-medicine-affiliate-program); [RevShare NASM](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/national-academy-of-sports-medicine-dec9388f); $25 claim: [LinkWhisper fitness programs](https://linkwhisper.com/fitness-affiliate-programs/), [InfluencerMarketingHub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/health-fitness-affiliate-programs/)
- **ISSA (on Impact):** the listings conflict:
  - **14.4% of online sales, 30-day cookie** — [FlexOffers ISSA](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/issa-international-sports-science-association)
  - **8% of sales + $8 per online lead, 30-day cookie** — [Lasso ISSA](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/issa-international-sports-science-association/)
  - **7.5% per sale + $10 per lead**, platform Impact — [LinkClicky ISSA](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/issa-international-sports-sciences-association/)
  - **5%** — [Shopper.com ISSA](https://shopper.com/partnerships/issaonline-com-affiliate-program)
  - ISSA is the only fitness brand with a reported **per-lead (CPL) component**.
- **NCSF:** official page states "Each individual and bundled certification program is eligible for a **15% commission**", confirmed in writing at registration. Free to join, application reviewed, sign-up via **app.impact.com**, paid monthly (about 30–60 days). Cookie not stated on the page. An aggregator claims 30 days, which is unverified. — [NCSF partners](https://www.ncsf.org/partners/); [LinkClicky NCSF](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/national-council-on-strength-and-fitness/)
- **AFPA:** **5% commission** on new student enrollments across 30+ certifications. Partners must be AFPA-certified and pass an application. An aggregator's 5–10% claim conflicts with the official page. — [AFPA affiliate partner](https://afpafitness.com/afpa-affiliate-partner)
- **ACE Fitness:** listed on FlexOffers with "commissionable courses" (CPT, group fitness, health coach, medical exercise). No rate was found. A directory says the cookie is "typically 30 days", which is unverified. — [FlexOffers ACE](https://www.flexoffers.com/?p=9417); [UpPromote ACE](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/ace-fitness/)
- **NESTA / Spencer Institute:** a press release (undated, MMA conditioning division) states "**30 percent** affiliate commission on the certification program as well as 30 percent on monthly membership fees", paid via PayPal. **Stale or unverified.** — [WebWire NESTA release](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=149255)
- **Trainer Academy / IPTA:** affiliate listing on **Impact** (and BlueAff under IPTA), with commission and cookie fields blank. IPTA exam with textbook costs about **$399**, or $799 for two certifications (may be outdated). — [affi.io Trainer Academy](https://affi.io/m/trainer-academy); [LinkClicky Trainer Academy](https://linkclicky.com/?p=13495); pricing context via [PTPioneer ACE CPT review](https://www.ptpioneer.com/ace-cpt-review/)
- **PTPioneer's disclosure** names **Commission Junction, Rakuten and Amazon Associates** ("possibly others"). It does not name the specific certification brands it earns from. — [PTPioneer disclosure](https://www.ptpioneer.com/disclosure/)
- **Precision Nutrition:** no public affiliate commission was found. Partner pages describe **partner pricing** (for example "additional $300 savings" for partner members), which is a buyer discount, not a referral payout. PN L1 is listed at about **$999** discounted by a reseller (may be outdated). — [HFA: PN announces seven new partners (2019, stale)](https://www.healthandfitness.org/?p=2277); [MovNat PN L1 listing](https://movnat.com/store/precision-nutrition-level-1-certification/)

### Inferences
- NCSF at 15% on certifications that typically cost several hundred dollars implies roughly **$50–$150+ per sale**. NCSF prices were not verified here, so treat this as an estimate. That likely makes NCSF the highest **verified** per-sale payout among fitness certifications. ISSA could be similar if the 14.4% rate is current, and it adds CPL ($8–$10 per lead). NASM at $4 flat is poor despite the brand's search volume.
- The fact that PTPioneer discloses CJ and Rakuten suggests some fitness certification programs (possibly NASM or ACE) run on those networks, but this was not confirmed.

### Gaps
- Not found: NSCA affiliate (NSCA is a membership association, and no program surfaced), **AFAA** (owned by NASM, so probably covered by NASM's program, unverified), Yoga Alliance schools, any nutrition coach program besides AFPA, and current **NASM/ISSA/ACE/NCSF certification list prices** from official sources.
- ACE rate unknown. ISSA's current rate is unresolved (5% to 14.4%). The NESTA 30% figure is undated.

## Q5. Which pay the most per sale, and which cover the most exams?

### Takeaway
No healthcare or fitness program found offers verified **high-ticket** payouts like $100+ per sale with public terms. The best verifiable programs are **NCSF (15%)**, **ISSA (up to 14.4% plus CPL)**, **Lecturio (15%)** and **Pocket Prep (15% recurring with a 45-day cookie, older terms)**. For **breadth**, the strongest are **Pocket Prep** (multi-certification app), **Kaplan** (NCLEX, MCAT, USMLE and more, but only about 5%), **Archer Review** (NCLEX, TEAS, FNP, USMLE, but gated) and **AFPA** (30+ fitness and nutrition certifications at 5%). High-ticket allied health (Penn Foster) requires a direct negotiation with the program manager.

### Cited Findings
- Ranking by verified commission rate: Lecturio 15% ([source](https://www.lecturio.com/en/elearning/more-info/affiliate)); NCSF 15% ([source](https://www.ncsf.org/partners/)); Pocket Prep 15% ([older official page](https://www.pocketprep.com/?p=7998), tiered now per [2026 page](https://get.pocketprep.com/become-an-affiliate/welcome)); ISSA 5–14.4% plus $8–10 CPL ([FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/issa-international-sports-science-association), [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/issa-international-sports-science-association/)); Nurse.com 10% ([source](https://www.nurse.com/scrub-squad/)); SimpleNursing 8% ([source](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/simplenursing-affiliate-program/)); Kaplan about 5% ([source](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/kaplan-test-prep-affiliate-program/)); AFPA 5% ([source](https://afpafitness.com/afpa-affiliate-partner)); NASM $4 flat ([source](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/national-academy-of-sports-medicine-dec9388f)).
- Longest cookie found: **Pocket Prep, 45 days** (older terms). The others are 30 days. — [Pocket Prep](https://www.pocketprep.com/?p=7998)
- Programs with **approval barriers:** Archer Review requires 1,000+ social followers ([source](https://www.archerreview.com/affiliates)). AFPA requires affiliates to be AFPA-certified ([source](https://afpafitness.com/afpa-affiliate-partner)). Penn Foster is manager-gated ([source](https://pennfoster.edu/affiliate/contact-us)). Pocket Prep excludes schools ([source](https://get.pocketprep.com/become-an-affiliate/welcome)).

### Inferences
- Suggested stack for testpreppilot.com (an inference, not sourced):
  1. **Impact account** covering NCSF, ISSA, Trainer Academy/IPTA, SimpleNursing and possibly Pocket Prep.
  2. **FlexOffers** for Kaplan, ACE and NASM.
  3. **Awin** for UWorld.
  4. Direct programs: Lecturio, Archer Review and Picmonic.
  5. A direct conversation with Penn Foster about CPL or host-post terms for medical assistant, phlebotomy, pharmacy tech and coding pages.
- Fitness pages (NCSF, ISSA) probably earn the most per conversion. Nursing and medical app or Qbank programs earn about $5–$45 per sale.

### Gaps
- No verified high-ticket ($1k–$4k) allied-health programme with public per-enrollment or CPL terms was found.
- Typical prices for most products (NASM, ISSA, ACE and NCSF certifications; Archer, Kaplan, Mometrix) were not verified in this pass.
- Many aggregator figures are undated. Re-verify every rate inside the network dashboard before relying on it.
