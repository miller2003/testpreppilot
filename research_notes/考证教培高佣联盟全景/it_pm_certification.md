# IT Certification and Project Management Exam-Prep Affiliate Programs (US Market, as of 2026-10-09)

Method note: about 23 web searches and fetches, run 2026-10-09. All currency is USD unless stated. **Official** means the brand's own affiliate page or FAQ. **Aggregator** means a third-party directory (FlexOffers, LinkClicky, affi.io, RevShare, UpPromote and similar). These directories often show stale terms, and in several cases below they contradict the official page. Each search returned a summary of its result pages; most numbers below come from those summaries rather than from reading the pages in full. The one exception is Tutorials Dojo, whose FAQ was fetched and read directly. Anything not found is listed under Gaps.

## Q1. Practice-test and course providers: commissions, cookies, networks, approval

### Takeaway
Among the practice-test vendors that publish terms, payouts are modest: 10–20% on tickets of roughly $15–$100. Tutorials Dojo pays 10% with a 7-day cookie. MeasureUp pays 10–20% on a tiered scale. Whizlabs pays about 12–15% (aggregators only). The broad-catalog platforms are the better fit for a site with many exams. Coursera pays 15–45% with a 30-day cookie, Pluralsight pays 50% of the first month or 15% of an annual plan, Simplilearn pays 10% capped at $290 per sale with a 30-day cookie, and KodeKloud pays 10–20% recurring with a 60-day cookie. Several large IT training brands have no public affiliate program I could find: CBT Nuggets, INE, Cybrary, Infosec Institute and Dion Training. LinkedIn sunset its affiliate programs, and ITProTV's FlexOffers listing is marked inactive.

### Cited Findings

**Tutorials Dojo (AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes practice exams)**
- Commission is 10% on every successful referral, with a 7-day cookie. Minimum payout is $30 USD, paid by PayPal on the 20th of the following month — [Tutorials Dojo affiliate FAQ (official, fetched)](https://portal.tutorialsdojo.com/faq-for-affiliate-marketing/)
- Free to join. The team reviews each application and sends a confirmation email (manual approval). Affiliates can generate deep links to specific products. Commission is reversed on refunds or cancellations — [Tutorials Dojo affiliate FAQ](https://portal.tutorialsdojo.com/faq-for-affiliate-marketing/)

**Whizlabs (broad catalog: AWS, Azure, GCP, CompTIA, PMP, Cisco and others)**
- Aggregator figures conflict:
  - FlexOffers lists 12% of sales and a 30-day cookie. The same page also promotes a 90-day window — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/whizlabs-com-affiliate-program/)
  - LinkClicky lists 15% per sale, via ShareASale, with a 30-day cookie — [LinkClicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/whizlabs/)
  - Shopper.com lists 5–10% — [Shopper.com](https://shopper.com/partnerships/whizlabs-com-affiliate-program)
- Whizlabs' own blog describes an "associate" affiliate role but gives no percentage — [Whizlabs blog](https://www.whizlabs.com/blog/become-a-whizlabs-associate-2/)
- Networks cited across listings: FlexOffers, Awin, ShareASale, BlueAff. No official rate was found.

**MeasureUp (official Microsoft practice tests; also CompTIA, PMI and others)**
- Tiered by sales volume. MeasureUp's documentation lists 10% (Bronze) for $1–$4,999 in sales, 15% (Silver) for $5,000–$14,999 and 20% (Gold) for $15,000 and up. The landing page says "up to 20%" — [MeasureUp affiliate page](https://www.measureup.com/become-affiliate); [MeasureUp docs](https://docs.measureup.com/?p=3440)
- Payout has a $50 minimum. The affiliate must submit an invoice and can request payment between the 1st and 10th of each month. Affiliates must be in a related sector and have a website, blog, database or social channel — [MeasureUp docs](https://docs.measureup.com/?p=3440)
- Affi.io lists a 10% flat rate, a 30-day cookie and a $67 average order value. That listing is for a UK TradeTracker campaign, so it may not reflect the US direct program — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/measureup)
- MeasureUp's materials did not say whether Boson-branded products are covered.

**Boson (Cisco CCNA practice exams)**
- I found no Boson affiliate program in my searches (gap).

**Pluralsight (absorbed A Cloud Guru)**
- Official terms: $5 per free-trial signup, 50% of the first month of a monthly subscription and 15% of an annual subscription — [Pluralsight affiliate page](https://pluralsight.com/affiliate)
- Network: several aggregators say Impact. One aggregator shows a CJ listing "since 13.09.2025" and no active Impact listing, so the network may have moved in 2025 — [Affilitizer](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/pluralsight.com); [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/pluralsight/)
- Cookie length conflicts: 45 days on one listing, 60 days on another — [Affilimate](https://affilimate.com/programs/pluralsight-affiliate-program/); [RevShare](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/pluralsight)
- The A Cloud Guru platform began its final shutdown on 2025-11-04, and all customers migrated to Pluralsight. Any standalone A Cloud Guru affiliate program should be treated as defunct — [Pluralsight release notes, Nov 2025](https://help.pluralsight.com/help/study-offline)

**Coursera (Google Career Certificates, IBM, Microsoft, PMI-aligned courses)**
- Official baseline is 15–45% on eligible purchases within 30 days of a click, with bonuses for strong performance. Eligible items are courses, Specializations, Professional Certificates and Coursera Plus. Runs on Impact. Subscriptions earn commission on the first month only — [Coursera affiliates (official)](https://coursera.org/about/affiliates)
- An aggregator breaks this down as 20% on individual courses, 45% on Specializations, Professional Certificates and monthly Coursera Plus, and 10% on annual Plus. Degrees are reportedly excluded. Third-party data, unverified — [Affilimate](https://affilimate.com/programs/coursera-affiliate-program/); [Referly](https://www.referly.so/affiliate-programs/coursera)

**Udemy (very large catalog of exam-prep courses, including popular instructors such as Andrew Ramdayal for PMP)**
- Official baseline is 10% of valid sales, with an increase based on performance. The cookie lasts 7 days and is not product-specific. Subscription purchases and purchases with unapproved coupon codes earn nothing. These help pages were last updated in 2023–2024 — [Udemy partner support: commission](https://partnersupport.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049412813-What-is-the-Commission); [Udemy: cookie](https://partnersupport.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049412573-What-is-a-Cookie-How-Long-do-Cookies-Last)
- Involve Asia's Udemy US listing shows 10.5% "until July 1", then 7% — [Involve Asia](https://app.involve.asia/directory/udemy-us-affiliate-program)
- UpPromote says new applications go through Impact as of 2026 — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-programs/udemy/)
- Otto's guide claims 15% and a 30-day cookie, which contradicts the official 7-day cookie — [Otto](https://joinotto.com/en-za/influencer-programs/udemy)

**Simplilearn (PMP, CAPM, ITIL, Six Sigma, AWS, Azure, CompTIA, cybersecurity bootcamps)**
- Official: 10% commission, up to $290 per transaction, 30-day cookie — [Simplilearn affiliate page](https://www.simplilearn.com/affiliate-program)
- Conflicting aggregators list 8% and a 45-day cookie (RevShare page updated 2026-08-31) — [RevShare](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/simplilearn-5a4a0046); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/simplilearn-affiliate-program/)
- Directories mention Impact, CJ, Admitad and FlexOffers as networks.

**KodeKloud (Kubernetes CKA/CKAD, DevOps, Linux, cloud)**
- Official commissions, paid for the life of the subscription: 20% recurring on monthly plans and 15% on annual plans for new customers, 10% for existing customers. The page's headline says 10–30%. The cookie lasts 60 days — [KodeKloud affiliates (official)](https://kodekloud.com/affiliates)
- Awin also confirms a 60-day attribution period. FlexOffers lists 16% — [Awin](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/35441); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/kodekloud-affiliate-program/)

**ACI Learning / ITProTV**
- FlexOffers lists $20 per paid subscription signup, up to $24 when sales exceed $2,000, and a 45-day cookie. The listing itself says "we are not currently offering this affiliate program in our system", so it is likely inactive or moved — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/itprotv-affiliate-program/)

**CBT Nuggets**
- No affiliate program found. Its individual annual subscription is $599 (consumer price, not a commission) — [search context, vertice.one](https://vertice.one/vendors/cbt-nuggets)

**LinkedIn Learning**
- A June 2023 LinkedIn Learning instructor deck includes an "Affiliate program sunset" slide, citing a decision to end affiliate programs across LinkedIn. Treat the program as closed — [LinkedIn instructor all-hands PDF, June 2023](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/learning/en-us/pdfs/june_2023_instructor_all_hands.pdf)

**INE and Cybrary**
- Neither publishes an affiliate commission. Both run reseller, MSP or academy partner programs instead: INE offers volume discounts and 25% off for academies, and Cybrary has a "Gold Partner" program with margins and rebates — [INE partnerships](https://ine.com/partnerships); [INE Academy Partner](https://learn.ine.com/enterprise-partnerships-academy-partnership-program); [Cybrary partner program](https://www.cybrary.it/partner-program)
- EC-Council has its own affiliate program for creators and educators. The rate was not shown — [EC-Council affiliate](https://affiliate.eccouncil.org/)
- A secondary French-language roundup says TryHackMe pays up to 5% through Impact (unverified) — [HackerDNA roundup](https://hackerdna.com/fr/blog/best-cybersecurity-affiliate-programs)

**Digital Cloud Training (AWS, Azure, GCP courses plus high-ticket Cloud bootcamps)**
- Tiered by confirmed referrals: 8% for 0–9, 10% for 10–29 and 12% for 30 or more. The 30-day cookie, $100 minimum payout and monthly USD payouts are from the official terms pages — [Digital Cloud Training affiliates](https://digitalcloud.training/affiliates/); [Terms](https://digitalcloud.training/terms-of-service-affiliate-program/)
- Commission is earned on up to the first 6 payments or 12 months, whichever comes first. The page puts earnings at $464–$695 per Cloud Mastery Bootcamp sale, on $5,790 tuition, which is the highest per-sale figure among the course and practice-test providers. Branded PPC bidding and coupon sites are banned without approval — [Digital Cloud Training affiliates](https://digitalcloud.training/affiliates/)

### Inferences
- A practice-test vendor at 10% on a $15–$40 order pays only $1.50–$4 per sale. Broad platforms with subscriptions or bundles pay much more per conversion. Simplilearn can pay up to $290, Coursera 45% on certificate products, and Pluralsight 50% of a first month (about $15–$25, depending on current pricing, which was not verified).
- Tutorials Dojo and Udemy use 7-day cookies, which hurts sites like this one, where readers research for weeks. Simplilearn and Coursera (30 days) and KodeKloud and Brain Sensei (60 days) are more forgiving.

### Gaps
- No official current Whizlabs rate was found; the rate exists only in conflicting aggregator listings.
- I found no affiliate program for Boson, ExamTopics, Professor Messer, Dion Training, TestOut, uCertify, Skillsoft / Global Knowledge, QuickStart, Learning Tree, New Horizons, Intellectual Point, Firebrand, Great Learning or KnowledgeHut / upGrad. They were not confirmed closed. For most, no affiliate page turned up, and searches were not run for each one individually because of the tool-call budget.
- Status of an A Cloud Guru-specific affiliate program before the shutdown is unknown. Linux Academy was absorbed earlier (via A Cloud Guru), and no standalone program was found.

## Q2. CompTIA's own store and voucher resellers

### Takeaway
I found no public affiliate program for CompTIA's store, exam vouchers or CertMaster. CompTIA's channel is a Partner / Authorized Academy program that gives discounted pricing to institutions, not commission to publishers. MeasureUp is the closest indirect route to monetizing official-style practice tests, but its CompTIA coverage was not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Search results show CompTIA's Partner Program and Authorized Academy Partner status give partners preferred or discounted voucher and CertMaster pricing, not affiliate payouts — [University of Richmond SPCS news](https://news.richmond.edu/news/article/-/14818/.html); [CompTIA blog on CertMaster](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/blog/empowering-instructors-and-improving-learner-success-with-certmaster-suite-learning-solutions/)
- ACM members get 20% off CompTIA vouchers, bundles and CertMaster, a member discount rather than an affiliate deal — [ACM member offers](https://learning.acm.org/member-offers/comptia)

### Inferences
- For CompTIA pages, the realistic monetization is third-party prep. Options include Pluralsight, Udemy, Coursera, Whizlabs and Simplilearn, plus MeasureUp if its CompTIA coverage is confirmed. Selling vouchers directly does not look like an option.

### Gaps
- No voucher reseller with a public affiliate program was found. It is not known whether CompTIA runs a private or influencer affiliate deal on Impact or CJ, because nothing was found publicly.

## Q3. PMP, CAPM, ITIL and Six Sigma prep providers

### Takeaway
PMP has a few real programs. OSP's PM PrepCast advertises 15% but requires a call and application before approval. Brain Sensei pays $50 (10%) on its $499.99 full course with a 60-day cookie. Simplilearn's 10% (up to $290) covers PMP, CAPM, ITIL and Six Sigma. Six Sigma options are thin: GoLeanSixSigma about 12% (aggregator), ExpertRating "up to 30%" (aggregator), and 6sigma.us case-by-case.

### Cited Findings
- **PM PrepCast / OSP International (PMP, CAPM, PMI-ACP, PDUs):** The page title says "Earn 15% Commission". Joining takes an email, then a get-to-know-you conference call, then an online application. The program targets established project-management websites. The cookie length was not visible — [PM PrepCast affiliates (official)](https://www.project-management-prepcast.com/affiliates)
- **Brain Sensei (PMP):** $50 per sale of the full PMP course priced at $499.99 (10%), with a 60-day cookie, via LinkConnector — [LinkConnector campaign](https://www.linkconnector.com/affiliate-campaign-description/?cid=6155)
  - FlexOffers instead lists 16% of sales — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/brain-sensei-affiliate-program/)
  - Affi.io lists the program on FlexOffers and CJ with a 60-day cookie — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/brain-sensei)
- **PM Solutions (online certification training):** $20 per sale, per FlexOffers (aggregator) — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/pm-solutions-online-certification-training-affiliate-program)
- **Simplilearn:** covers PMP, CAPM, ITIL and Six Sigma at 10% up to $290 with a 30-day cookie (see Q1) — [Simplilearn](https://www.simplilearn.com/affiliate-program)
- **Six Sigma:**
  - GoLeanSixSigma: 12% with a 30-day cookie (FlexOffers listing) — [FlexOffers GLSS](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/glss-goleansixsigma-com-affiliate-program/)
  - ExpertRating: "up to 30%", covering project management and Six Sigma — [FlexOffers ExpertRating](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/expertrating-affiliate-program/)
  - 6sigma.us: "the percentage of commission may vary for each case" — [6sigma.us partners](https://www.6sigma.us/partners.php)
  - Six Sigma Online (Aveta, now an Educate 360 brand): no affiliate program found — [sixsigmaonline.org about](https://www.sixsigmaonline.org/about-us/)

### Inferences
- For the PMP and CAPM pages, PM PrepCast at 15% and Brain Sensei at $50 per sale are the most PMP-specific options. Simplilearn has the highest payout ceiling per sale ($290 at 10%, implying $2,900+ courses), but it only reaches that ceiling on high-ticket purchases.

### Gaps
- No affiliate terms were found for Project Management Academy, PMTraining, Velociteach, TIA Education or ASQ. Andrew Ramdayal / TIA is monetizable only indirectly, through Udemy (10%, 7-day cookie).
- PM PrepCast's cookie length and payout terms could not be retrieved.

## Q4. Cybersecurity and tech bootcamps: referral or affiliate payments

### Takeaway
Most big-name bootcamps do not run open affiliate programs, and the evidence that remains is mostly referral bonuses. Springboard pays a $250 referral bonus. Flatiron is reportedly on Rakuten with a 10-day cookie, but no rate was found. No affiliate program was found for Fullstack Academy, Coding Dojo, Thinkful or Infosec Institute. The best-documented high-ticket bootcamp payout is Digital Cloud Training at 8–12% of $5,790 tuition, or $464–$695.

### Cited Findings
- **Springboard:**
  - The referral program gives the referrer $250 and the referee $750 off a Career Track. It excludes Intro, Prep and university-partner courses. The official blog post was updated 2024-11-04 — [Springboard referral program](https://www.springboard.com/blog/news/springboard-referral-program/)
  - An older FlexOffers listing shows a tiered 24%/16%/8%/4% payout and a 30-day cookie, but says it is "not currently offering this affiliate program". Likely discontinued — [FlexOffers Springboard](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/springboard-affiliate-program/)
- **Flatiron School:** affi.io shows the program closed on FlexOffers and open on Rakuten, with a 10-day cookie and no rate shown. One vendor blog claims $500 per enrolled student, which is unverified — [affi.io Flatiron](https://affi.io/m/flatiron-school); [GrowSurf examples](https://growsurf.com/examples/coding-bootcamp-referral-programs/)
- **Infosec Institute:**
  - No affiliate program found. It sells through EdAssist employer partnerships (a 30% tuition discount for EdAssist client employees) and partner landing pages — [Training Industry press release](https://trainingindustry.com/press-release/it-and-technical-training/infosec-institute-expands-partnership-with-edassist-to-remove-learning-cost-barriers-and-improve-employee-participation-in-its-education-offerings/)
  - Its boot camp plus certification voucher package is valued at up to $4,999 — [Infosec Accelerate 2024](https://infosecinstitute.com/newsroom/2024-infosec-accelerate-award-program/)
- **Fullstack Academy, Coding Dojo, Thinkful:** no affiliate or referral program found. The only Thinkful item is a 2019 reader discount of $600 off, which is stale — [Technical.ly 2019](https://technical.ly/dc/2019/01/18/online-coding-bootcamp-thinkful-is-offering-technical-ly-readers-600-off-courses/)
- **Digital Cloud Training bootcamps:** $464–$695 per Cloud Mastery Bootcamp sale (see Q1) — [Digital Cloud Training affiliates](https://digitalcloud.training/affiliates/)

### Inferences
- High-ticket boot camps ($3k–$5k, such as Training Camp, Infosec, QuickStart and Firebrand) seem to sell through sales teams, employer tuition programs and B2B partners rather than public affiliate links. Any deal would probably have to be negotiated directly, as a CPL or rev-share contract.

### Gaps
- Training Camp, Intellectual Point, Firebrand, QuickStart and Learning Tree: no affiliate or CPL terms found. My search for a "Training Camp" affiliate program returned only Digital Cloud Training.
- 2U/edX bootcamps: not researched. Note that 2U went through Chapter 11 in 2024; this is general knowledge and was not sourced here.
- Coursera's affiliate offer covers Google certificates (see Q1); degrees are reportedly excluded.

## Q5. Highest $ per sale and broadest IT exam coverage

### Takeaway
**Highest $ per sale (documented):**
1. Digital Cloud Training bootcamp: $464–$695.
2. Simplilearn: up to $290.
3. Brain Sensei: $50.
4. PM PrepCast: 15% of a ticket that was not verified.

**Broadest coverage per approval:**
- Coursera (15–45%, 30-day cookie, Impact)
- Udemy (10%, 7-day cookie, Impact)
- Simplilearn (10%, 30-day cookie)
- Pluralsight (50% of first month or 15% of annual)
- Whizlabs (about 12–15%, aggregators only)

Together, Simplilearn, Coursera and Pluralsight (or Udemy) cover nearly every exam family on testpreppilot.com: Azure, CompTIA, CCNA, AWS, GCP, ITIL, PMP/CAPM and Six Sigma.
