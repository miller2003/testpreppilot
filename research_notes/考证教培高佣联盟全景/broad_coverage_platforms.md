# Broad-Coverage (Multi-Exam) Test Prep & Certification Affiliate Programs — US Market (research date 2026-10-09)

Scope: single affiliate programs that could each cover dozens to hundreds of the ~477 exam guides on testpreppilot.com. Currency notes: "official page" = brand's own affiliate page fetched 2026-10-09 (most show only a © 2026 footer and no effective date). "Directory" = third-party aggregator (FlexOffers listing pages, Awin merchant profile, affi.io, Affilimate, LinkClicky, UpPromote, Lasso, etc.). Directory numbers often conflict and may be stale; each one is flagged.

Category codes used below: RE = real estate; CON = contractor (CSLB etc.); IT = IT certs; ACC = CPA/EA/CFA/accounting/finance; NUR = nursing/health/medical assistant; FIT = personal trainer; TRD = CDL/OSHA/trades; INS = insurance license; CIV = civil service/police/corrections/firefighter/postal; K12/ADM = PSAT/SAT/ACT/GRE/GMAT/LSAT/MCAT/AP; TCH = Praxis/teacher certification; LEG = legal; PM = PMP.

## Q1. Which multi-exam brands have affiliate programs, and on what terms? (commission, AOV, cookie, network, status, restrictions)

### Takeaway
The confirmed broad-coverage, high-commission programs are **Mometrix (30%+, in-house)**, **JobTestPrep (20%+, Awin/in-house, 7-day cookie)**, **TestPrep-Online (up to 25%, sister site of JobTestPrep)**, **Exam Edge (15%, Awin, 60-day cookie, 1,800+ exams)**, and **Pocket Prep (15% recurring, 45-day cookie, Impact)**. Big brands such as Kaplan, Princeton Review, McGraw Hill, Pearson and Study.com pay lower rates (3–12%) but on high order values. I found **no affiliate program** for Union Test Prep, Test Prep Review, Smart Edition, 240 Tutoring, Career Step, Gleim, Practice4Me, StudyGuideZone or Uniprep.

### Inferences
- Mometrix's 30% in-house program is the single highest-rate program with near-universal exam coverage. Its catalog spans most of the site's categories (nursing, teacher, civil service, real estate, insurance, PMP, fitness, admissions). The unknown cookie length and in-house tracking are the main risks.
- JobTestPrep and TestPrep-Online appear to share an operator. Together they cover civil service, police, corrections, employment tests and K-12 entrance tests at 20–25%. The 7-day cookie (Awin) is short, so links should be placed close to the purchase decision.
- Exam Edge has the longest confirmed cookie (60 days) among the high-% programs and claims 1,800+ exams. At a $52 average order and 15%, that is about $7.80 per sale. Mometrix at 30% of a typical study guide earns more per sale, but its order values were not found.
- Kaplan, Princeton Review, UWorld and Magoosh pay 6–12% of high order values ($990–$1,200+ average where reported), so a single sale can be worth $60–$140. However, they cover mainly the admissions and nursing slice of the site.

### Gaps
- Mometrix cookie length, eligible products, typical order value and tracking platform are not published. Ask during the application.
- JobTestPrep official cookie length is not stated on its own page (Awin says 7 days).
- Pocket Prep's current base % under the new tiered program is not published.
- Udemy's current baseline (10% vs 15%) and LinkedIn Learning's actual rates could not be confirmed from primary sources.
- Union Test Prep's site blocked fetch, so a footer affiliate link cannot be fully ruled out.
- No sourced product price lists for Mometrix, JobTestPrep or Pocket Prep were collected.

## Q3. Which offers the best "coverage × commission" combination?

### Takeaway
Rank: **(1) Mometrix** (30%+, widest catalog, in-house, cookie unknown) → **(2) JobTestPrep/TestPrep-Online** (20–25%, civil service, police and K-12 pages that other brands don't monetize) → **(3) Exam Edge** (15%, 60-day cookie, 1,800+ exams, Awin) → **(4) 360training** (15%, CJ, real estate, OSHA and insurance) → **(5) Pocket Prep** (15% recurring, 45 days). Kaplan, Princeton Review, UWorld and Magoosh have the best per-sale dollars on admissions, accounting and nursing pages, but at a lower rate and with narrower coverage.
