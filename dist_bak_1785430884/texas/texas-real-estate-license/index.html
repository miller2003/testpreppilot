Your Route in Texas

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Complete 180 hours of TREC-approved pre-license education.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Submit your application to TREC along with the required fees.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Get fingerprinted and pass a background check.

  4. 4

    Step 4

    Pass the Texas Real Estate Sales Agent exam (National and State portions).

  5. 5

    Step 5

    Find a sponsoring broker to activate your license.

State FAQ

Do I need a degree to get a Texas real estate license?

No, a college degree is not required. You only need to complete the 180 hours of approved pre-license education.

Can I take the exam online?

No, the Texas real estate exam must be taken in person at a Pearson VUE testing center.

How long does the background check take?

Background checks usually take a few weeks to process, but can take longer if you have a criminal history.
